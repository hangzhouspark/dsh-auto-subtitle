Artificial intelligence is changing how we work, but the biggest gains do not come from replacing people. They come from removing the small frictions that eat up a working day. A developer who spends twenty minutes hunting for a configuration file loses more than twenty minutes, they lose the thread of what they were doing. Tools that remember context and surface the right file immediately give that thread back. The same logic applies to meetings, code review, and customer support. None of these wins are dramatic on their own, but they compound. Over a quarter, the accumulated effect is a team that ships more and argues less. That is the real return on investment, and it is easy to miss because no single moment feels like a breakthrough.
